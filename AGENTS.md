## Project-specific rules

1. Current north star: **Cinematic · Intellectual · Experimental** digital museum / visual-culture exploration.
2. Research and design direction must be updated before broad implementation changes.
3. Open-source code may be copied/adapted only after license/provenance is verified and logged.
4. Never copy a reference's complete visual identity. Extract behavior and rebuild with LUMEN tokens/content/IA.
5. Use feature branches and PRs. Do not merge to `main` until relevant build/browser/a11y/visual gates pass.
6. A successful build is not visual QA. Inspect rendered pages at desktop/tablet/mobile.
7. Do not claim user-validation or product metrics that were not actually measured.
8. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; every spatial/motion-heavy surface needs a usable fallback.
9. Prioritize one coherent signature system over many unrelated effects.
10. When a visual QA failure is caused by art direction, repair the direction/system instead of stacking CSS patches.
