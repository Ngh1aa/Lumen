# LUMEN Round 01 — Screener + Consent

## Preferred participants

Recruit people who:
- browse art, photography, design, museums, cultural media or visual archives at least occasionally;
- sometimes explore without a specific artist/work already in mind;
- can complete a 25–35 minute prototype session;
- do not need professional art-history expertise.

Useful diversity:
- different levels of art familiarity;
- at least one participant who regularly uses keyboard navigation or prefers reduced motion, when feasible.

## Screener

1. How often do you browse museums, art/design websites, photography archives or visual-culture content?
2. When you browse, are you usually looking for a specific thing or exploring without a precise target?
3. Have you used digital museum/gallery experiences before? Which type, broadly?
4. Do you normally use mouse/touch, keyboard, reduced-motion settings, or another interaction preference we should support?
5. Are you willing to complete a 25–35 minute fictional/portfolio prototype session?
6. Are you willing to let anonymized behavioral observations be used in a portfolio case study? Optional.

## Consent script

Confirm:
- LUMEN is an independent experimental digital-museum prototype;
- participation is voluntary and can stop at any time;
- notes use participant IDs rather than names;
- recording requires separate explicit permission;
- portfolio use of anonymized evidence requires separate explicit permission;
- aesthetic taste is not being graded; the study is about comprehension, discovery and orientation.

## Evidence classification

Participants matching the exploration behavior above may be treated as `DIRECT_USER` for the concept audience. Designer-only critique without target browsing behavior should be logged separately as peer critique, not direct-user validation.
