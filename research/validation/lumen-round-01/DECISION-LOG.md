# LUMEN Round 01 — Decision Log

## D-01 — Parallel discovery modes

- **Current decision:** Keep Drift, Grid, Color and Mood as parallel ways to enter the collection rather than forcing one browse model.
- **Evidence state:** `WORKING_PROTOTYPE + HYPOTHESIS`
- **Risk:** Choice may create orientation cost before discovery begins.
- **Evidence that could change it:** Participants cannot predict mode behavior, repeatedly choose a mode that conflicts with intent, or abandon for search immediately.
- **Next evidence:** Task 1.

## D-02 — Relationship Atlas as continuation, not a separate visualization toy

- **Current decision:** Use artwork relationships as a next-step path from detail, connecting works by artist/era/movement/color/mood relationships.
- **Evidence state:** `WORKING_PROTOTYPE + HYPOTHESIS`
- **Risk:** The graph/relationship language may be visually compelling but semantically weak or disorienting.
- **Evidence that could change it:** Participants cannot explain why works are connected or lose their place when following a relationship.
- **Next evidence:** Task 2.

## D-03 — Motion should carry orientation but remain optional

- **Current decision:** Use motion to reveal/connect/orient while preserving keyboard and reduced-motion semantic paths.
- **Evidence state:** `WORKING_PROTOTYPE + ACCESSIBILITY_TARGET`
- **Risk:** Motion may create meaning that disappears when reduced, or may distract from collection content.
- **Evidence that could change it:** Reduced-motion users lose equivalent information/function, or participants repeatedly misread spatial transitions.
- **Next evidence:** Task 4.

## D-04 — Lightweight Saved Collection

- **Current decision:** Let people save works and return without adding account-heavy collection management to the concept.
- **Evidence state:** `WORKING_PROTOTYPE + HYPOTHESIS`
- **Risk:** Save feedback/collection location may be too subtle, making the feature feel temporary or unreliable.
- **Evidence that could change it:** Participants do not notice save confirmation, cannot find saved work again, or expect stronger organization.
- **Next evidence:** Task 3.

## Update rule

Only promote decisions from hypothesis toward direct-user evidence when session artifacts and atomic evidence IDs exist. A changed interaction is an **iteration**, not an improvement, until retested.
